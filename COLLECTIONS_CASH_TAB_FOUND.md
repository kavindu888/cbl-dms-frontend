# Collection session Cash tab verification

## What exists

- `CollectionSessionDetailPage.jsx` imports `CashTab` from `src/components/collections/PaymentTabs.jsx` and renders it for the `cash` tab. The session header and summary sidebar remain separate and unchanged by the Cash-tab implementation.
- The denomination grid ends with the `Total cash` table footer in `CashTab`. Immediately after that table, the allocation panel is conditionally rendered when the calculated cash total is greater than zero.
- `CustomerSelector.jsx` exists and accepts `value`, `onChange`, optional `placeholder`, and optional `routeId` props.
- `InvoiceAllocationTable.jsx` exists as a shared component. It supports manual allocations, caps allocations at the outstanding amount, displays partial/overdue invoices, and auto-allocates oldest invoices first.
- `getOutstandingInvoices(customerId, params)` exists in `src/api/collectionsApi.js` and calls `GET /api/v1/collections/outstanding-invoices` with `customerId` as a query parameter.
- `useOutstandingInvoices(customerId)` exists in `src/hooks/useCollections.js`. It is disabled until a customer ID exists and does not retry a rate-limited response.
- `CashTab` owns customer and allocation state, invokes `useRecordCashPayment()`, and calls `mutation.mutateAsync(...)` from its submit button.
- Successful submission resets denomination counts, the selected customer, and allocations, then refreshes the session.
- Submission is disabled until the cash total is positive, a customer is selected, at least one allocation exists, and the allocation total matches the cash total.

## What was missing

Nothing from the requested Cash-tab flow is currently missing. The original assertion was based on an older version of the page. In the current implementation the allocation UI is intentionally hidden until at least one denomination produces a cash total greater than zero.

## Rate-limit note

The repeated `GET /api/v1/sales/invoices/outstanding` responses shown in the supplied log were generated by the Sales invoice-payment page's previous per-customer request fan-out. That flow has been replaced with a single organization-wide invoice-list request. The Collections Cash tab uses the Collections outstanding-invoices endpoint and makes one request after customer selection.
